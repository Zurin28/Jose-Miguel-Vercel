# 💡 General AI Event Takeaway Tips

- **Use Multimodal References:** Feed screenshots and visual examples directly to AI models to anchor design and layout expectations.
- **Leverage `.md` Files for Context:** Use structured Markdown files (like `summary.md` and `CLAUDE.md`) to establish baseline context without wasting token budgets or triggering redundant searches.
- **SOP to Automation:** Turn repeatable tasks into structured rules, then into reusable agent skills.
- **Smart Boss / Cheap Workers:** Use frontier reasoning models to plan and orchestrate, and smaller fast models for high-volume execution.
