# 🤖 Agentic Workflow: The Future of Intelligent Automation — Google Antigravity

## 🎯 Keynote: Larry Mark Somocor

## 🔍 The Shift to Autonomy (Chapter One)

### ⚠️ The Limits of Traditional Automation
* **Traditional RPA** — Deterministic automation with rigid scripts lacking contextual reasoning.
* **Standard LLMs** — Single-turn prompting and conversational interfaces.
* **Agentic Workflows** (The Next Frontier):
  * Composing multiple **specialist agents**.
  * Dynamic goal pursuit, self-correction, and tool invocation.

### ❓ What Makes a Workflow "Agentic"?
* **Reasoning** — Decomposing complex, ambiguous objectives into executable subtasks.
* **Tools** — Granting agents file system, terminal, browser, and API access.
* **Memory** — Retaining conversational and project state across multi-step execution.
* **Collaboration** — Agent-to-agent delegation and user co-piloting.

* The future of work is **AI and you vs. the problem**.
