# 🎨 World Premier — Design & Style Reference Guide

> [!NOTE] Purpose
> This guide documents the **exact visual language** used across all World Premier (WPP7) marketing materials I produced. Use this as a reference when designing manually in Canva/Photoshop, or as a **prompt guide when using AI image generators** (e.g., Midjourney, Adobe Firefly, ChatGPT image gen, Gemini) to create WP-branded graphics.

| **Gilded Ivory / Pure White** | Body text, subheadings, warm readable copy | `#FAF7EE` / `#FFFFFF` |

### Secondary / Contextual Colors

| Name | Usage | Hex Approximation |
|---|---|---|
| **Charcoal** | Cards and secondary dark surfaces | `#1E2421` |
| **Graphite** | Supporting text on light surfaces | `#4A524D` |
| **Sage Mist** | Soft muted panels | `#DCEBDD` |

### Color Rules
- ✅ **Green + Gold** = default WP brand pairing (used in 70%+ of materials)
- ✅ **Dark bg + white text + gold accent** = standard layout formula
- ✅ Gradient backgrounds always go **dark-to-lighter** (top-dark / bottom-lighter, or left-to-right)
- ❌ Avoid neon, pastel, or cool-toned palettes — they clash with the brand
- ❌ Never use flat solid green alone — always add gradient depth

## 3. 🔤 Typography System

### Core Brand Typefaces

- **Poppins Bold:** Main headlines and prominent speaker names.
- **Montserrat Semibold:** Subheadings, labels, metadata buttons, and uppercase pills.
- **Lora Regular:** Supporting narrative copy and editorial-style body text.

### Font Style Observations & Rules

| Role | Preferred Font & Style | Feel / Guidelines |
|---|---|---|
| **Main Event Title / Hero Text** | **Poppins Bold** / Display serif | Maximum 1 primary text gradient (Royal Gold to Champagne); bold, impact. |
| **Brand Name "World Premier"** | Clean semi-bold sans-serif, title case | Premium, readable, authoritative. |
| **Speaker Name & Title** | **Poppins Bold** (Name) + **Montserrat** (Title in caps) | Clean, grouped; **never** place leaf icons or random badges next to name. |
| **Supporting / Body Text** | **Lora Regular** or clean sans-serif | Generous line-height, solid ivory/white, high legibility. |
| **Uppercase Metadata Labels** | **Montserrat Semibold**, wide tracking | Date, Day, Time, Platform (concise pills). |
| **Footer / URL / Social** | Small, light-weight sans-serif | Subtle, balanced, functional. |

### Typography Rules
- **Hero text** is always the strongest typographic focal point.
- **Mix 2–3 font weights/families max** across any design (strictly Poppins, Montserrat, and Lora).
- **Never distort, warp, or stretch type.**
- **No 3D, extruded, beveled, or glowing text.** Gradients on text are strictly limited to one key word or headline—never applied to body text or multiple subheadings.
- High contrast: Pure White or Gilded Ivory on dark backgrounds; Antique Gold or Charcoal on light surfaces.

## 4. 📐 Layout Patterns by Poster Type

### 🟢 Type A — Event / Speaker Poster
*(Examples: "A Night With The Chairman", "Health Is Wealth — Zamboanga")*

```
┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│  [Logo top-right corner]        │
│  [Cut-out speaker photo, left]  │
│  [Guest photo, right, overlaps] │
│                                 │
│  ┌──────────────────────────┐   │
│  │  BLACK PILL: "event tag" │   │
│  └──────────────────────────┘   │
│  ┌──────────────────────────┐   │
│  │  GOLD PILL: BIG TITLE    │   │  ← Largest element
│  └──────────────────────────┘   │
│  [Name in Gold text]            │
│  [Date | Time | Platform CTA]   │
│                                 │
│  ──── Footer: URL | Facebook ── │
└─────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Key techniques:**
- Speaker photos are **cutout (background removed)**, placed over the bg
- Subject faces always visible, cropped at waist or full-body
- 2 pill-shaped text blocks stacked (black pill for label, gold pill for title)
- Black footer bar with white text + social icons

- "World Premier Testimonies" script heading in top-left
- Green gradient bg with subtle darker overlay

## 8. 🚫 Anti-AI Gloss Rules — "Do NOT Make It Look Like Shiny AI"

> [!CAUTION] Critical Rule
> World Premier posters must look like **professional Canva/Photoshop graphic design work** — bold, clean, warm, and human. They must **never** look like CGI renders, 3D glossy stock art, or that telltale hyper-polished AI aesthetic. The target audience (Filipino community members) responds to trust and authenticity — not synthetic perfection.

### What the "Shiny AI Look" Is (And Why to Avoid It)

The default AI image generation aesthetic produces:
- 🪞 Overly smooth, waxy, plastic-looking skin and surfaces
- ✨ Excessive specular highlights and fake lens flare
- 🔆 Unnaturally even, "studio softbox" lighting with no shadows
- 🎨 Over-saturated, HDR-pumped colors with artificial contrast
- 🤖 Backgrounds that look like 3D renders or Unreal Engine scenes
- 👤 People who look like flawless digital mannequins, not real humans

The WP poster style is **graphic design first** — real photos of real people, bold flat typography, solid color blocks, and brand-consistent layout. It is **not** AI-illustrated art.

---

### ✅ What WP Posters Actually Look Like (Stay True To This)

| Element | Correct WP Style |
|---|---|
| **People** | Real cutout photos — natural skin, real expressions, no AI face generation |
| **Backgrounds** | Flat gradient fills or blurred real photography — not 3D renders |
| **Lighting on photos** | Natural, documentary, slightly warm — not studio glamour |
| **Colors** | Brand greens and golds — muted, not neon or over-saturated |
| **Textures** | Flat design surfaces, slight grain on bg — not glass, metal, or CGI sheen |
| **Typography** | Bold, clean, graphic — not "glowing" or embossed 3D type |

---

### 🛑 Banned Visual Shortcuts & Effects (The "Shiny AI" & Template Traps)

Never include these elements in prompts, layout drafts, or final artwork:

- **Lighting & Flairs:** Lens flares, sparkles, glitter, glowing outlines, light streaks, artificial spotlights, or dramatic rim lights.
- **Surface & Finish:** Chrome, mirror-like metal, glossy gold, specular reflections, plastic/waxy textures, or metallic sheens.
- **Dimensionality:** 3D typography, beveled, embossed, inflated, or extruded lettering, heavy drop shadows, or stacked shadows.
- **Decorative Clutter & Space-Fillers:**
  - Random floating dots, bokeh particles, geometric dust, or abstract shapes placed merely to fill empty space.
  - **No leaf icons or leaf symbols** placed beside names, titles, or headers (specifically: **never** place a leaf icon next to Chairman/CEO Danilo S. Ramos Jr.'s name panel).
  - Excessive nested frames, multiple borders, or arbitrary rounded rectangular boxes around every element.
  - Unnecessary ribbon banners, decorative seals, or fake interface pills that don't convey distinct metadata.
- **Typography & Details:**
  - Stretched, warped, or distorted typefaces.
  - Slogans or decorative flourishes invented just to plug visual gaps.
  - Unofficial or re-colored brand logos.

---

### ✅ Restrained, Human-Directed Techniques (Use Instead)

- **Palette & Contrast:** Flat color fields, restrained dark backgrounds (e.g., `#0B3D1E` Royal Forest to `#14633A` Imperial Emerald), and warm ivory (`#FAF7EE`) or crisp white text.
- **Subtle Gradient Rule:** Smooth, low-contrast background gradients (broad and gradual, not banded). Text gradients must be strictly limited to **one major headline or key word** (e.g., restrained Royal Gold `#D4A62A` to Champagne `#EBD28B`), with all other text solid.
- **Clean Structural Hierarchy:**
  - Generous negative space—empty space is intentional breathing room, not a void to fill.
  - Thin, purposeful dividers only when strictly separating distinct data groups (like date vs. time).
  - Consistent corner radii and border weights.
  - Open space mixed with a minimal number of structured panels rather than putting everything inside boxes.
- **Authentic Portrait Treatment:** Preserve recognizable human features, natural skin textures (pores, matte finish), and documentary lighting. No synthetic facial smoothing or cutout halos.

### 🔧 Platform-Specific Anti-Gloss Settings

#### Midjourney
- Append `--style raw` → disables Midjourney's internal beauty/gloss engine
- Append `--s 50` or `--s 100` → lowers stylize value; less AI polish (default is 100–250)
- Append `--no shiny, glossy, plastic, waxy, cgi, 3d render, airbrushed, HDR` at the end
- Example suffix: `--style raw --s 75 --no glossy, plastic, 3d render, airbrushed`

#### Stable Diffusion / Leonardo AI / Fooocus
Paste into the **Negative Prompt** box:
```
plastic, waxy skin, glossy, shiny, polished, slick surface, synthetic,
airbrushed, smooth skin, flawless skin, CGI, 3D render, Unreal Engine,
Blender render, video game graphic, over-saturated, high contrast, HDR,
glowing highlights, reflections, metallic sheen, photorealistic render
```
Emphasize the worst offenders: `(plastic skin:1.3), (glossy sheen:1.2), (CGI:1.4)`

#### Adobe Firefly
- Use the **Style Reference** panel — upload an actual WP poster image as reference
- Set the **Visual Intensity** slider to low-to-medium (avoid maxing it out)
- Use explicit material descriptors in prompts: `"matte finish"`, `"flat graphic design"`, `"warm natural tones"`

#### ChatGPT / Gemini Image Gen
- Describe the medium explicitly: `"in the style of a professional marketing flyer, not a 3D render"`
- Add: `"flat graphic design aesthetic, matte colors, no glossy effects, no CGI"`
- Reference Canva: `"designed in Canva style, clean bold typography, solid gradient background"`

### 🎯 The One-Line Rule to Remember

> **"This should look like it was made in Canva or Photoshop by a skilled Filipino graphic designer — not generated by an AI art bot."**

If the output looks like it belongs in a video game cinematic or a luxury skincare ad — it's wrong. If it looks like something you'd print and hang at a WP event — it's right.

## 8. ✅ Design Checklist (Before Finalizing Any Poster)

- [ ] It looks professional, premium, restrained, and intentional (not flashy or templated)
- [ ] WP logo badge placed using official asset (never distorted, redrawn, or placed over busy backgrounds)
- [ ] Primary text is legible at thumbnail size and on mobile screens (test at 200px wide)
- [ ] Color palette conforms to brand standards (Royal Forest, Imperial Emerald, Royal Gold, Champagne, Ivory/White)
- [ ] Background gradient is broad, subtle, and low-contrast
- [ ] Any text gradient is restricted strictly to a single headline or highlight word
- [ ] Footer bar present with official website (`www.worldpremierintl.com`) + `World Premier Ph`
- [ ] Person photos are cutout with clean edges, natural skin texture, and no glowing rim halos
- [ ] **NO leaf icons/symbols** placed next to the CEO/Chairman name or titles
- [ ] No more than 2–3 font styles used (Poppins Bold, Montserrat Semibold, Lora Regular)
- [ ] Gold accent is used sparingly on the most critical focal point—not splashed everywhere
- [ ] **NO "Shiny AI" artifacts:** zero chrome, zero glossy reflections, zero 3D text/bevels, zero lens flares, zero floating particles/dots
- [ ] Negative space is preserved; empty areas are not cluttered with filler shapes or decorative boxes
- [ ] Factual event details (date, day, time, Zoom platform) are copied accurately without alterations
- [ ] Every element on the canvas serves a distinct communication purpose
