## 3. 🏗️ Distribution Model

World Premier uses a **three-tier distribution hierarchy**:

```
Company (WPP7 Trading)
    └── Hub Operators (City-level exclusive distributors — PHP 500,000 fee)
            └── Mobile Stockists (Sub-distributors under hubs)
                    └── Members / Customers (End buyers)
```

### Hub Operator Role
- Awarded **exclusive territorial distributorship** per municipality.
- Earns per package sold and per member registered.
- Must set up a physical Hub Office within 90 days of signing MOA.
- Operates a **computerized system** (PHP 25,000 activation fee) for transactions.
- Governed by a formal **Memorandum of Agreement (MOA)** with WPP7 Trading.

### Mobile Stockist Role
- Sub-distributor under a Hub Operator.
- Sources products from Hub at Hub Price; sells at Member/Non-Member price.
- Also bound by a formal **Mobile Stockist MOA**.

## 4. 💰 Compensation / Business Model

World Premier operated a **Binary + Uni-Level income plan** (evolved over time):

| Earning Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Uni-level Package Income** | Commission per package sold per level in downline |
| **Binary Income** | Earnings based on paired/balanced downline structure |
| **Hub Profit** | Per-package margin earned by hub operators from distributors |
| **Mobile Stockist Profit** | Margin between hub price and member price |
| **Raffle / Incentive Prizes** | Cash prizes (up to PHP 50,000), package consolation prizes, account packages (Platinum/Copper) given in company raffles |

> [!IMPORTANT] Regulatory Note
> The company documented a formal **90-Day Money-Back Guarantee** policy, referencing compliance with consumer protection law and requiring licensed healthcare provider verification for refund claims. Refunds processed in weekly installments over 60 days.
