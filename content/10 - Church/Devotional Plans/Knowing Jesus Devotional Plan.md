# Knowing Jesus & Becoming Like Him — 28-Day Reading Plan

**Anchor verse (KJV):** 2 Corinthians 3:18 — _"But we all, with open face beholding as in a glass the glory of the Lord, are changed into the same image from glory to glory, even as by the Spirit of the Lord."_

**Word study:** "changed" = _metamorphoō_ (G3339) — a real, inside-out transformation (same root as "metamorphosis"). Not acting like Jesus — becoming like Him. Same word Paul uses in Romans 12:2 for the renewed mind.

**How this works:** You beholding = you reading. One chapter = one look at His glory. Over 28 days, that adds up to real change, not overnight but steady, like water shaping a rock.

**Daily format (10–15 min):**

1. Read the chapter (7–10 min)
2. Answer the day's reflection question in your notes app or notebook (3 min)
3. One-sentence prayer turning what you read into a request (1–2 min)

---

**Testimony for the road:** George Müller read through the whole Bible over 200 times in his life, and said the more he did it, the more his prayer life and love for God deepened — not because reading is magic, but because beholding changes you (2 Cor 3:18). Consistency over intensity.

**If you miss a day:** Don't restart. Just pick up the next chapter. Grace, not guilt — that's the whole point of OSAS living out in daily habits too.
